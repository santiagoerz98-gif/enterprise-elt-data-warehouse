### Business Questions

The final analytical dataset should answer the following questions:

1. **What are the total sales and number of orders over time?**

   * Analyze sales performance by day, month, quarter, and year.

2. **Which products generate the most revenue?**

   * Identify top- and bottom-performing products based on sales quantity and revenue.

3. **Which customers contribute the most to total sales?**

   * Analyze customer purchasing behavior and revenue contribution.

4. **Which employees generate the most sales?**

   * Compare sales performance across employees and identify top performers.

5. **Which categories and suppliers perform best?**

   * Analyze revenue and product sales by product category and supplier.

These questions will guide the **dbt dimensional models and final analytical tables** in the DuckDB warehouse.
